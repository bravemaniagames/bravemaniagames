How to join

The in-game server browser is the simplest way in, on both Steam and Epic.

  1. Launch ARK: Survival Ascended and choose Join Game.
  2. Select Unofficial servers, with the mode set to PvE or All.
  3. Tick Show Player Servers.
  4. Search for Bravemania or BraveRevo. You can also try Brave or Braver.
  5. Refresh once or twice, then choose your map from the BraveRevo PvE Cluster list.

Steam

Use the server browser first. The forum lists the 27000-range addresses as Steam join addresses, but that port may be a browser or query port and not the game port, so it may not work as a direct connection.

Epic and direct connections

Use the Epic / direct address, which carries the game port. If your in-game console is available, enter open braverevo.ddns.net:7777, swapping 7777 for your map's Epic / direct port. This may not work on every platform.

Xbox and PlayStation

The listing names Steam and Epic, and console access isn't confirmed here. Please ask on Discord before planning to join from a console.

If a map feels laggy after joining

Secure your items, then respawn with your Survivor Implant: press E to open your inventory, hover over the implant and press E again. Respawning at your base is safest.

What no-wipe means here

No-wipe covers your long-term progression. It doesn't mean nothing is ever removed.

No planned full-cluster wipes

The cluster runs as a long-term server, with no planned reset of everyone's progression.

Wild dinos are wiped routinely

Automated daily updates include wild dino wipes. These are routine maintenance and don't reset the cluster.

Inactive bases and dinos can decay

Smart Decay clears out inactive structures and dinos to keep the maps clean, so a base isn't guaranteed to stay forever if you stop playing. Ask on Discord for the current timers and rules.
